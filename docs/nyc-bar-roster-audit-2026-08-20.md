# NYC gay-bar roster audit

Checked: **2026-08-20**
Scope: all 83 records in `NYCGayBars/Data/Resources/bars.json`, plus a five-borough search for missing LGBTQ+/queer-focused venues.
Method: desk research only; no venue was telephoned. First-party venue pages and current event calendars were preferred. Where those were unavailable, recent independent reporting, NYC/NYS records, and current venue directories were used. A missing or stale listing was not treated as proof of closure.

## Executive summary

- **73 current records have current operating evidence** and do not need a closure action.
- **Five more operators/venues are active, but their records reflect a move, reopening, or stale status label/address:** G Lounge, The Boiler Room, The Duplex, Rawhide, and Adonis Lounge.
- **Bar Nine meets the agreed permanent-closure standard** and is the sole high-confidence archive/removal candidate. No roster change has been made; user confirmation is required first.
- **MB Bar Boutique remains temporary/uncertain.** A current LGBTQ venue directory calls it temporarily closed, while a late-2025 NYS permit record still names the corporation. There is no credible reopening plan in the evidence found.
- **VV Bar & Kitchen and Alibi Lounge have conflicting or insufficient evidence** and should remain unchanged pending stronger verification.
- **PRIDE Bar & Lounge remains forthcoming/uncertain.** The premises and operators are documented, but no official opening date was found.
- **FairyTale Lounge is user-approved for addition** at 500 W 48th St, using the user-confirmed display spelling and a new ID separate from the archived Adonis record.
- **Six strong addition candidates** meet the agreed LGBTQ focus or sustained-programming standard: Oberon, Solera, Athena Keke's, Wilka's, Love Thy Neighbor, and The Exley.
- No qualifying dedicated venue was found in Staten Island; the best-known lead is merely gay-popular, which does not meet the inclusion rule.

## Evidence and confidence conventions

- **High:** current first-party hours/events or an official opening announcement.
- **Medium:** recent independent reporting or multiple current listings, but no useful first-party status page.
- **Low:** single directory/community lead or conflicting evidence; no data action recommended.

Broad cross-checks used for the operating roster include a [current August 2026 NYC LGBTQ event calendar](https://www.gayout.com/usa-canada/united-states/new-york/events?when=2026-08), [Thotyssey's July 10, 2026 listings](https://thotyssey.com/2026/07/10/friday-7-10-2026/), [Thotyssey's July 18, 2026 listings](https://thotyssey.com/2026/07/18/saturday-7-18-2026/), [Time Out's June 2025 LGBTQ bar guide](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/lgbt/best-gay-bars-nyc), and [Eater's June 2025 queer bars and restaurants guide](https://ny.eater.com/maps/best-gay-bars-nyc-queer-lesbian-lgbtq-restaurants-pride). Venue-specific first-party or government sources are called out where they materially change the result.
