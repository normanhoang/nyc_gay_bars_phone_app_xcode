Frequently asked

Does the app need internet access?
Only for the optional Friends feature (which uses iCloud). Everything else runs and is stored locally on your device.

How do I add a friend?
Open the Friends tab, set a display name, and share your invite code — as text, as a tappable link (share button), or as a QR code your friend scans with the iPhone Camera. Scanning the QR opens the app straight to your request (it works even with no signal). If they don't have the app yet, the shared link points them to the App Store. Once they accept, you're connected. Friends requires being signed in to iCloud.

Can I choose which friends see my check-ins?
Yes — each friend row in the Friends tab has a paper plane toggle controlling whether that friend receives your shared check-ins at all. Friends' check-ins show up in the Tonight section of the Friends tab; check-ins don't send push notifications.

How long do shared check-ins last?
Friends see them in the Tonight list for about 6 hours; the records are deleted entirely within about 24 hours.

Is my data backed up if I get a new phone?
Data is stored in local app storage on-device only, so it does not transfer automatically. There is currently no export/backup feature.

How do I request a bar be added or corrected?
Email the address above with the bar name and neighborhood.
